# Cabin surface refinement provenance

This package modifies the existing original authored GT-R LAB cabin. It does not contain Nissan CAD, a downloaded cabin model, scanned textures, or photograph-derived pixels. Visual comparisons used the official 2024 US Nissan GT-R brochure and the Nissan 2024 setup-switch close-up. Those copyrighted reference pixels are deliberately excluded from the deliverable.

The new leather, fine fabric and console-twill maps are generated by the included deterministic numpy/scipy/Pillow script. The thin physical control markings are original typesetting using DejaVu Sans and independently drawn simplified symbols. The font software, license and deterministic rebuild are retained with the source backup. The text/icon shapes remain visual approximations and do not imply functional controls or an exact factory reconstruction.

The two pre-existing steering badge components remain unchanged. They adapt GTR logo_Red_0 and GTR logo_Metal_0 from Ciasny's already-accepted exterior, source https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/nissan-gtr-r35-51c912a8310c4e00a82ad7673d84228a, license CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. The existing derivative fitted and depth-flattened this geometry onto the steering hub. No Nissan endorsement is implied. No exterior geometry or exterior textures are included in or changed by this pass.

The recorded color, roughness, metallic, grain-size and texture values are authored rendering approximations, not measured Nissan properties. The cabin remains an original work-in-progress visual approximation. Factory geometry, working instruments, seat/door movement and physical-device performance are not certified or added.
