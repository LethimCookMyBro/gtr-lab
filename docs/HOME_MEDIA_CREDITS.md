# GT-R LAB photo credits

Each photo is a real camera photograph. Model year, vehicle specification and capture date are distinct. The collection combines period photographs with modern photographs of historic cars and replicas. Captions preserve that distinction.

The 1969 GT-R is the PGC10 four-door sedan; the KPGC10 two-door hardtop followed in 1970. Model year, depicted specification and photograph capture date are separate. This collection combines period photographs with modern photographs of historic cars and replicas; each caption identifies the distinction.

The eight `archive-` additions were checked against their Wikimedia Commons file descriptions and the downloaded originals on 2026-10-03. Their entire source frames are retained. They are optimized by proportional downsampling and WebP compression only, without AI enhancement, retouching, added aging or color grading. Original sRGB/ICC profiles are retained when present; EXIF and XMP metadata are stripped. No original is enlarged.

Every image is linked to its own entry on `/credits#<asset-id>`. Source, author, license, capture date, framing context and each display copy’s change notice remain accessible there. All binary dimensions, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `src/data/home-media-credits.json`.
